Good questions

A few things you might be wondering.

What should I have ready before I begin?

Your PAN details and the mobile number linked to your Aadhaar. We’ll remind you before verification, so you don’t get stuck halfway.

What if identity verification takes a while?

We’ll explain that your request is still processing. You can leave the app and return when it’s ready.

Is investing without risk?

No. Investments are subject to market risks, and returns are not assured. Read the relevant scheme documents before deciding.
